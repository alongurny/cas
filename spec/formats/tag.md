# Format: tag

The tag of a release: a version as Semantic Versioning 2.0.0 writes it
(`semver` in [types.md](../types.md)), such as `1.4.0`, `2.0.0-rc.1` or
`1.0.0+build.5`. That is three numbers joined by dots, each without
leading zeros; then optionally `-` and dot-separated identifiers of
letters, digits and hyphens, where an identifier of digits alone has no
leading zeros; then optionally `+` and dot-separated identifiers of
letters, digits and hyphens.
